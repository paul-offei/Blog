Technical communication is a critical skill set for what we do at TexkLab. No matter how innovative or productive a scientist may be, the value of their work depends on their ability to communicate to colleagues, clients, regulators, and the general public. 

So, as part of the onboarding process, we will be taking an online workshop on Technical Communication for 14 days. 

The workshop is intended to equip interns with foundational skills to concisely and logically write and present technical or scientific work. And how to use a reference  management  software.
